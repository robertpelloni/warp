# VISION: The Ultimate LLM Harness

## Core Foundation
Warp Rebuild is a Go-based evolution of the Warp Agentic Development Environment. It aims to be the most powerful, performant, and extensible "harness" for LLM-powered development, born from the terminal but expanding into a comprehensive workspace.

## Foundational Concepts
1. **Agentic at the Core:** Unlike traditional terminals where AI is an add-on, Warp Rebuild is designed around the concept of autonomous and semi-autonomous agents working alongside the developer.
2. **High-Performance Terminal:** Leveraging Go's concurrency and efficiency to provide a low-latency, robust terminal experience.
3. **Universal Integration:** Incorporating the best features from every major AI coding tool (Tabby, Hyper, Wave, Codex, Claude, Gemini, etc.) into a unified, coherent experience.
4. **Cross-Platform & Native:** Providing a high-quality, native-feeling experience across macOS, Linux, and Windows.
5. **Open & Extensible:** A modular architecture that allows for easy integration of new LLMs, tools, and workflows.

## User-Satisfaction Design
- **Frictionless Onboarding:** Instant setup with sensible defaults.
- **Context Awareness:** Deep understanding of the user's project, history, and current task to provide the most relevant AI assistance.
- **Transparent Autonomy:** Users can see and control exactly what agents are doing, ensuring trust and reliability.
